## Explicit gaps and limitations

- Accounting, credit, funding and capital coverage varies by bank/period; use the metric-level CSV rather than interpreting blanks as zero.
- BTG NPL90 is not normalized; corporate and consumer credit risk require separate definitions.
- Historical workbooks are normalized from the latest available mapped vintage. Earlier documents are archived; their full metric restatement deltas are not yet normalized. Caixa PDF comparative vintages are retained in pdf_versions.json.
- Itaú historical spreadsheet is currently an explicit 2Q26 supplement; a subsequent-quarter refresh must discover and validate its replacement link.
- Same-URL remote replacement detection requires the full recheck option; normal incremental mode checks local hashes and discovers changed/new catalog IDs/URLs.
- Narrative interpretation is reviewed only through 2Q26. Future quarters require a new evidence review.
