# Caixa Econômica Federal — investigative lens

These are questions to investigate, not predetermined findings or permanent judgments. Update the analysis as the evidence changes.

## Franchise questions

- Housing/mortgage concentration, duration, funding sources and policy-linked economics; distinguish the bank's balance sheet from funds it administers.
- Savings, term deposits, credit letters and other funding: pricing, maturity and dependence on housing funding mechanisms.
- Retail/commercial PF/PJ, infrastructure and agribusiness: contribution, loss concentration and portfolio weights; a small distressed book can have a high ratio without characterizing the whole bank.
- Government services, payments, cards, insurance distribution and asset management as fee franchises; quantify scale, monetization and expense absorption.
- Recurring versus accounting profit, provisions/write-off criteria, legal/actuarial items, capital requirements and distributions.

## Known data traps

- ROE and efficiency in the key-figures tables are trailing 12 months.
- Quarter, YTD and annual values coexist on the same PDF page; superscript footnotes can be mistaken for numbers.
- Customer deposits, broad funding and administered assets have different scopes; FGTS administered assets are not automatically bank funding.
- Call transcripts and thematic reviews are available under data/calls and the bank knowledge directory; consult latest_calls.json for exact coverage and outstanding gaps.
