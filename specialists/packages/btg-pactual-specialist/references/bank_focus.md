# BTG Pactual — investigative lens

These are questions to investigate, not predetermined findings or permanent judgments. Update the analysis as the evidence changes.

## Franchise questions

- Business-line economics: investment banking, corporate lending, sales/trading, asset management, wealth/personal banking, consumer finance and interest/other results.
- Recurring management fees versus performance/transactional revenues, client flows versus market appreciation, and the quality of AuM/WuM monetization.
- Pan and other acquisitions: compare original/perimeter-adjusted history, integration effects, goodwill and capital use; isolate organic growth.
- Corporate and consumer credit quality separately, concentrations, guarantees, collateral and underwriting; do not infer low risk from an absent retail NPL metric.
- Funding mix, maturity, secured/unsecured funding, market risk, RWA intensity, liquidity buffers and capital allocation between franchises.

## Known data traps

- AuM/WuM are client assets, not bank assets or revenue; avoid unsupported double counting.
- Balance-sheet loans differ from the corporate-plus-consumer business portfolio.
- Reported operating expenses include items excluded from adjusted efficiency; reported expenses/revenue does not reproduce that ratio.
- Call transcripts and thematic reviews are available under data/calls and the bank knowledge directory; consult latest_calls.json for exact coverage and outstanding gaps.
