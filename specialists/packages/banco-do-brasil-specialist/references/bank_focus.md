# Banco do Brasil — investigative lens

These are questions to investigate, not predetermined findings or permanent judgments. Update the analysis as the evidence changes.

## Franchise questions

- Agribusiness and rural-credit economics: client concentration, subsidized/equalized lines, repricing, collateral, restructurings and loss migration; distinguish policy mechanisms from unsupported assumptions about political intervention.
- Public-sector relationships, payroll/retail, corporate and SME franchises; test how client/product mix changes margins and funding stability.
- Credit recoveries, discounts and provisioning alongside NPL and coverage; distinguish a quarterly profit rebound from a credit-cycle inflection.
- Treasury, insurance/affiliates, pension-related contributions and tax effects; reconcile their role in recurring and accounting results.
- Capital generation/distribution and expected-loss transition: separate Resolution 4,720 legacy tables from CMN 4,966 presentation.

## Known data traps

- Some spreadsheet cells are BRL displayed as millions; others are thousands or already millions.
- Agribusiness is separately classified in selected credit tables; do not add it to PF/PJ figures that already include it.
- Published narrative, current workbook and original quarter can use different expanded-credit definitions.
