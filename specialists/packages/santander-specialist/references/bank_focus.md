# Santander Brasil — investigative lens

These are questions to investigate, not predetermined findings or permanent judgments. Update the analysis as the evidence changes.

## Franchise questions

- Client-margin changes through mass-market selectivity, consumer financing, SME/corporate, product spreads and liability mix; test deliberate risk reduction versus lost competitiveness.
- Funding composition, PF/PJ mix, customer primacy, pricing and maturity; connect to margin and liquidity outcomes.
- Wholesale-specific provisions, retail/consumer vintages, restructurings and revised write-off criteria; reconcile NPL movements with provisioning and Stage 3.
- Cards, insurance, consortia, brokerage and other fees versus lending dependence; measure profitability and distribution strength where disclosed.
- Managerial/accounting reconciliation, goodwill exclusions, exceptional provisions, expense discipline, taxes and capital return.

## Known data traps

- ROAE excludes goodwill; the accounting-profit base differs from recurring managerial profit.
- Legacy 2024 sheets use thousands where current tables use millions.
- Stage 3 coverage is not NPL90 coverage; certain historical ratios exclude extraordinary provisions.
- A falling NPL ratio alongside rising provisions needs migration/write-off/denominator analysis, not an automatic improvement verdict.
