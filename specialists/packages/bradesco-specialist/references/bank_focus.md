# Bradesco — investigative lens

These are questions to investigate, not predetermined findings or permanent judgments. Update the analysis as the evidence changes.

## Franchise questions

- Banking versus insurance/pensions/capitalization: isolate revenue, underwriting/claims, financial income, capital and profit contributions.
- Transformation execution: distribution footprint, high-income/SME client propositions, digital engagement, technology costs and realized productivity.
- Client versus market NII, liability margins, secured origination and SME collateral realization; test risk-adjusted returns rather than equating collateral with safety.
- Provisions, Stage 3 migration, restructurings, recoveries and credit cost across the recovery cycle.
- Recurring versus accounting profit, fiscal/labor adjustments, administrative expense reclassifications and capital distribution.

## Known data traps

- The mapped historical ROE is YTD annualized; quarterly ROAE in releases is a different series.
- Managerial and accounting balance sheets have different consolidation perimeters.
- Insurance strength does not by itself establish improvement in the banking franchise; broader operating expenses are not a common peer denominator.
